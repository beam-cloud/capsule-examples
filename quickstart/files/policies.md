# Company Knowledge Base

## Access

- Request production access in the #access-requests channel.
- Include the system name, reason, manager approval, and requested duration.
- Access to customer data requires security approval.

## People Operations

- PTO requests go through the HR system and should be added to the team calendar.
- Expense reports are due by the fifth business day of the next month.
- Customer travel over $1,000 requires manager approval before booking.

## Customer Work

- Document customer escalations in the account notes before posting in Slack.
- Include owner, deadline, customer impact, and the next customer-facing update.
- Escalations affecting revenue or security should be flagged to leadership immediately.
