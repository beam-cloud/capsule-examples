# Market Structure Notes

Modern financial markets are fragmented across venues, dark pools, and dealer networks.
For liquid equities, price discovery is continuous and public. For many credit and rates
products, liquidity is still relationship-driven and more episodic.

Important ideas:

- Liquidity is not just volume. It also depends on spread, depth, resilience, and how much
  price impact a trade creates.
- A market maker earns spread but carries inventory risk when client flow is one-sided.
- Latency matters most where the same instrument or close substitutes trade on many venues.
- End users care about execution quality, auditability, and the ability to explain why a
  trade was routed a certain way.
