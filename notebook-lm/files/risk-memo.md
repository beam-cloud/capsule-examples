# Risk Memo

Portfolio risk is usually easier to reason about when split into market risk, liquidity
risk, credit risk, operational risk, and model risk.

Signals to watch:

- Concentration: a portfolio can look diversified by ticker while still being concentrated
  in a single factor, geography, or funding source.
- Liquidity mismatch: liquid liabilities funded by illiquid assets can force sales at the
  worst time.
- Hidden leverage: derivatives, financing terms, and correlated collateral can increase
  effective exposure faster than headline positions suggest.
- Model drift: a model that worked in one volatility regime can fail when correlations
  compress or funding assumptions change.
