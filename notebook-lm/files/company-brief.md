# Company Brief

Northstar Markets is a fictional research platform for portfolio managers. It combines
market notes, uploaded filings, analyst memos, and internal commentary into a single
workspace.

Product principles:

- Sources first: every answer should point back to the documents that support it.
- Research flow: users should be able to ask questions, generate study artifacts, and keep
  working without leaving the page.
- Human control: summaries are drafts. A portfolio manager or analyst owns final judgment.
- Auditability: the app should preserve source context so decisions can be reviewed later.
